# Surface brief — NutriLife app (all screens)

Scope: the whole mobile-first app (landing "/", cliente, nutrizionista, cucina, admin, etichette). Mode: Operate (the "/" landing leans Persuade but stays a single screen).

Audience/job: patients ordering tomorrow's weighed meal in under a minute; kitchen staff reading production and printing labels; nutritionists composing meal templates; the owner administering.

Constraints: Italian UI, standard web controls, allergens always in text, large tap targets, high contrast. Labels page stays white for printing.

## Direction contract

THESIS: "Cucina 3.0" as a night-mode instrument panel for food: precise numbers glowing on black, meals as coloured data. Refuses the pale-green wellness template.

OWN-WORLD: Ground #0B0B0B, dark cards #1A1A1A, white text; full pastel fills with black text — lime #EDFF8C (what matters most), mint #CFE1CA (ok/example), sky #D8DFE9 (meal templates), light #EFEFEF; frosted glass (white/8 + blur) for header, icon buttons and the floating total bar. Urbanist: light 300 large for titles and numbers, 500–600 for labels. Radii 28px cards, pills everywhere, round icon buttons.

STORY: the visitor sees their week, each meal as coloured composition and price, orders before 18:00 knowing allergens, picks up a weighed meal.

FIRST VIEWPORT: sticky glass header (white fused-circles mark + NutriLife, role glass pill, round logout); huge light title; task cards below, primary action as a white pill; the decisive number in a lime card.

FORM: pinned by the owner (Behance "Sisense AI Assistant" as world reference; logo and graphics original). Signature move: every meal drawn as fused circles (protein rose, carb lime, veg green) sized by real grams. seed: none (pinned) — "riproduci stessi contrasti, glass effect, colori pieni, sfondo nero, considera tutto" (owner, 24/11/2026).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
