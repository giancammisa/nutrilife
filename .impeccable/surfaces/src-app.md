# Surface brief — NutriLife app (all screens)

Scope: the whole mobile-first app (landing "/", cliente, nutrizionista, cucina, admin, etichette). Mode: Operate (the "/" landing leans Persuade but stays a single quiet screen).

Audience/job: patients ordering tomorrow's weighed meal in under a minute; kitchen staff reading production and printing labels; nutritionists composing meal templates; the owner administering.

Constraints: Italian UI, standard web controls, allergens always in text (never colour only), large tap targets, high contrast (older patients).

## Direction contract

THESIS: "Cucina 3.0" — a neighbourhood kitchen run with lab precision. Refuses the category default of soft-green wellness cards with leaf icons and pastel gradients.

OWN-WORLD: Paper ground #F3F2EE, ink #121210, butter yellow #F4E96B as committed fields (header band, number cards, secondary buttons), sage #3E7A55 for vegetables/ok. Archivo: expanded 125% extra-heavy uppercase for headings, numbers, grams and prices; Archivo 100% for body. Pill chips for states and allergens; 14px card radius; ink pill primary buttons.

STORY: the visitor sees their week, sees each meal as its real composition and price, orders before 18:00 knowing allergens, picks up a weighed meal.

FIRST VIEWPORT: yellow header band with the fused-circles mark + NUTRILIFE wordmark left, role pill + Esci right; under it the page title in expanded caps; then the task list (days, orders, production) with the primary action as a full-width ink pill.

FORM: pinned by the owner (Behance "Omnui" direction as world reference; logo and graphics original). Signature move: every meal drawn as three fused circles (protein ink, carb yellow, veg sage) sized by real grams. seed: none (pinned) — "andiamo questa direzione ed usa le skill … per riprodurre fedelmente questa brand identity" (owner, 24/11/2026; copying of logo/artwork excluded by agreement).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
